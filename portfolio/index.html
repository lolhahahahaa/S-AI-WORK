Projects

Built at a hackathon

School portal for students

A school website where students track their own work in one place, instead of chasing it across notebooks and group chats. It grew into several tools inside one portal:

  • Tasks and deadlines to see what's due and what's late
  • Catch Me Up builds a recovery plan after a missed day
  • Peer Help Exchange matches classmates who can help each other
  • Lost and Found for reporting and claiming items
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
